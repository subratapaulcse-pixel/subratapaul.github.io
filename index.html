Curriculum Vitae

Studying how social networks form, evolve and hold together over time.

Subrata Paul is an Assistant Professor in the Department of CSE–AI at Brainware University, Barasat, and a PhD candidate (thesis submitted) at MAKAUT. His work sits at the intersection of dynamic social network analysis, computational intelligence and rough set theory, and spans 98+ publications across journals, conferences, book chapters, books and patents.
