About

Subrata Paul is an Assistant Professor in the Department of CSE‑AI at Brainware University, Barasat. His research asks how social networks evolve — how communities form, split, and re-form, and how hierarchy and influence shift over time in dynamic graphs. This is the subject of his PhD thesis, “An Investigation on Hierarchical Structures of Dynamic Social Network,” submitted at Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (formerly WBUT), Kalyani.

Alongside this core focus, his published work spans federated learning in healthcare, IoT and smart cities, machine learning for security and fraud detection, and applications of graph theory to problems from vaccine-delivery logistics to disaster response. He has authored or co-authored 4 books, 28 journal articles, 22 conference papers, 28 book chapters, and 8 filed patents, and regularly reviews for IEEE- and Springer-sponsored conferences.
